**Warzone 2100 Mod: More Fortress**

New Fortress:

Assault Gun fortress:
- Entry Level Fortress, only require Assault Gun and Wall 3 Research
- Same range as Cannon Fortress
- Lower HP than Cannon Fortress

Laser Fortress:
- Required Wall 5 + Pulse Laser Research
- Long Range, fires in rapid pulses and takes time to reload
- Very good against Cyborgs
- Mid against VTOL
- Mid against Light Body rox
- Poor against Medium Body Tanks (or stronger)
- Can be defeated by Arty or rushing with Medium Tanks


Usage:
Put the folder into mods/wz-version/multiplay
